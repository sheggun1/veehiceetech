Veehicee flagship education technology

Meet VICORA CBT.
Examinations, reimagined.

VICORA CBT is a computer-based testing solution built to help schools plan, conduct and manage digital examinations. It is designed to support question-bank organisation, examination setup, candidate access, timed test delivery, automated objective marking and result management. Its development direction covers web access, Windows desktop use and Android local-host workflows, giving schools flexible options for delivering assessments in different technology environments. The aim is to make examination administration more organised, reduce manual marking effort and provide a smoother testing experience for candidates and administrators.
