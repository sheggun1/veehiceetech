Work that makes
a difference.

Explore a selection of digital solutions shaped around real organisational needs. Our work includes school administration tools, communication workflows and planning systems. Each project begins with understanding the people who will use it, then turning their daily tasks into a clearer and more manageable digital process.

School Portal

School Portal — a school-focused digital workspace for academic administration, score entry, result access and parent engagement. It is designed to make school information easier to manage and easier for families to follow.

Communication Platform

A business communication platform concept designed to bring contact lists, campaign preparation and message workflows into one organised workspace. Its foundation supports future email, SMS and WhatsApp provider connections.

School Timetable System

A timetable planning tool that helps schools organise classes, teachers, subjects and fixed periods while accounting for combined lessons and scheduling constraints.
